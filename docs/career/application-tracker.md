# Application Strategy & Tracker

> **The #1 lever most people ignore:** *how* you apply matters more than *how many*. A
> referral is 2–3× more likely to get a callback than a cold application. Your plan:
> **referrals first, targeted applications second, cold job boards last.** And apply while
> you prep — early interviews are free practice.

## Referral engine (do this — it's the highest-ROI activity)

1. **Search LinkedIn** for 2nd-degree connections at target companies (ex-colleagues, college alumni from Anand International, people who liked your posts).
2. **Alumni network** — Anand International College grads at product companies are warm intros.
3. Ask for a referral the *right* way (template below) — make it effortless for them.

### Referral request template (LinkedIn / WhatsApp)
> Hi [Name] — hope you're doing well! I'm exploring senior full-stack roles and saw
> [Company] has an opening for [Role] ([link]). I've spent 4 years shipping production
> systems — 3 live products, 2 npm packages, WebRTC/fintech/AI-agent work. Would you be
> open to referring me? I'll send my resume + a 3-line blurb so it's zero effort for you.
> Totally understand if not. Thanks either way! 🙏

### Cold recruiter / hiring-manager outreach (when no referral exists)
> Hi [Name], I'm a full-stack engineer (4 yrs) who ships end-to-end — I've built and
> launched 3 SaaS products and 2 npm packages, with production WebRTC, multi-tenant fintech,
> and LLM-agent systems. I saw [Role] at [Company] and it lines up closely with what I do.
> Portfolio: shivamgupta.live. Would love to be considered — happy to share more.

## Negotiation play (Month 6 — worth 20–40%)

1. **Never say your current CTC first.** If asked, deflect: "I'd prefer to focus on the value
   I bring and the market range for this role — I'm looking in the 24–30 LPA band." (If a
   form forces a number, use *expected*, not current.)
2. **Never accept the first offer on the call.** Say: "Thank you, I'm excited — can I take a
   day to review the full details?"
3. **Run processes in parallel** so you can say "I have another offer at X" truthfully.
4. **Negotiate total comp**, not just base — base, joining bonus, ESOPs, retention.
5. Use market data: levels.fyi (India), AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, and any competing offer.
6. **Get every offer in writing** before resigning anywhere.

> Your current 8.4 is your weakest card — keep it face-down. Your products, system design,
> and any competing offer are your strong cards. Lead with those.
