# LinkedIn Optimization — Make Recruiters Find You

> You already did name-SEO on shivamgupta.live. LinkedIn is the *other* half: it's where
> recruiters actively search. An optimized profile + light posting flips you from
> "applicant" to "inbound" — recruiters start messaging *you*, which also improves your
> negotiating position. ~2 hrs to set up, 30 min/week to maintain.

---

## 1. Headline (the highest-impact 220 characters)

**Don't** use just "Full-Stack Software Engineer." Recruiters search by keywords + it's your
pitch. Use one of these:

> **Full-Stack Engineer (4y) · Node/NestJS · Next.js · Python · AI/LLM Agents · Shipped 3 SaaS products + 2 npm packages**

or, keyword-dense for search:

> **Software Engineer | Full-Stack (React, Node.js, NestJS, Python) | System Design · WebRTC · LLM/AI Agents · Building & shipping production SaaS**

Rules: front-load the searchable keywords recruiters filter on (React, Node.js, NestJS,
Python, System Design, AI). Include "shipped products" — your differentiator.

---

## 2. "Open to work" (recruiter-only mode)

Turn on **Open to Work → "Recruiters only"** (the green banner is optional; the private
signal is what surfaces you in recruiter searches). Set:
- Titles: Software Engineer, Full-Stack Engineer, Backend Engineer, SDE II/III, Senior Software Engineer.
- Locations: Remote (India) + Bangalore + Hyderabad + Pune + Jaipur (widen to not get filtered out).
- Start date: immediately / flexible.

---

## 3. About section (first 2 lines are what shows before "see more")

> I'm a full-stack engineer who **ships products end-to-end** — 3 live SaaS products
> (pSEO.cloud, Resite.live, a Shopify App Store app) and 2 published npm packages in 4 years.
>
> I work across the stack: React/Next.js on the front, Node/NestJS and Python/FastAPI on the
> back, PostgreSQL/Redis for data, and Docker/CI for shipping. I go deep on hard systems —
> production WebRTC/Mediasoup, offline-first edge↔cloud sync, multi-tenant fintech on a real
> financial ledger, LLM-agent orchestration, and algorithmic-trading engines.
>
> Recently: core engineer on a deployed disaster-response comms platform (showcased at ATR
> Open House 2025, Kyoto). Portfolio → shivamgupta.live
>
> Open to senior full-stack / backend roles at product companies. Let's talk.

Keep it first-person, scannable, keyword-rich. End with a call to action.

---

## 4. Featured section (visual proof — most people skip this)

Pin as "Featured":
- shivamgupta.live (your portfolio)
- pseo.cloud, resite.live, the Shopify app listing
- npm: uace-mcp, streamverse
- Any post that performs well

This turns your profile into a portfolio recruiters can click through.

## 5. Experience & Skills
- Mirror your **resume v2** bullets (with metrics) into the Experience section.
- **Skills:** add the top ~15 (LinkedIn ranks you in recruiter search by these). Prioritize:
  Node.js, NestJS, React, Next.js, TypeScript, Python, PostgreSQL, System Design, WebRTC,
  REST APIs, Microservices, Docker, Redis, LLM, AWS (if applicable).
- Get **endorsements** on the top 5 skills (ask a few colleagues — quick reciprocal favor).

---

## 6. Posting cadence (4 weeks) — build inbound + a "brand"

You don't need to be an influencer. **One good post/week** for a month makes you look active
and gives referrers/recruiters a reason to engage. Post from your real work:

- **Week 1 — Build-in-public:** "I shipped pSEO.cloud — here's the multi-LLM router with
  failover I built and why 3rd-party LLM reliability is harder than it looks." (technical,
  specific, screenshot).
- **Week 2 — Lesson learned:** "What building offline-first edge↔cloud sync for a
  disaster-response platform taught me about designing for failure."
- **Week 3 — Open source:** "I published UACE, an MCP server that gives AI coding assistants
  persistent project memory — here's the problem it solves." (link npm).
- **Week 4 — System design mini-thread:** "How I'd design a scalable video-calling system —
  P2P→SFU scaling, drawn from building StreamVerse." (doubles as interview prep!).

Rules: lead with a hook line, keep it skimmable, add one visual, end with a question to
drive comments. Engagement → more recruiter impressions.

---

## 7. Weekly maintenance (30 min)
- [ ] Accept/relevant-connect with 5–10 people at target companies (warms future referrals).
- [ ] Comment thoughtfully on 2–3 posts from engineers at target companies (visibility).
- [ ] Respond to any recruiter InMail within 24h — even a "not now, but keep me in mind."
- [ ] Post once (from the cadence above or your real weekly work).

---

## Success signal
By end of Month 2, you should be getting **inbound recruiter messages**. If not, your
headline/skills keywords need tuning — iterate the headline first (biggest lever).
